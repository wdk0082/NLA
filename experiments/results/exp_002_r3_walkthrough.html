NLA Metrics Walkthrough II

The alignment question of EXP001 asked of a much larger natural-language autoencoder, on clean in-distribution contexts, with every edit written by hand at the level of the whole explanation: page 2 follows five activations through every operation, page 3 gives the population results over all 256.

What changed since EXP001

The model

The community NLA for Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B (64 hybrid blocks, d = 5120), extraction after block 42. The verbalizer is the base with a merged warm-start LoRA plus one RL adapter; the activation is added, norm-matched, to the residual stream at block 1 rather than written over an embedding.

The data

FineFineWeb documents from astronomy, biology, economics, history. Every context starts at the first paragraph of running prose and ends at a whole word drawn uniformly at least 50 tokens later, at most 256 tokens in.

The edits

Every edit is a whole-explanation edit, hand-written: a phrase-level polarity flip (every claim denied, words kept), a full paraphrase and a translation; code does the “cat” edit, the shuffle, the unrelated swap and the unrelated swap that keeps the final token.

How data flows

The formulas, as implemented

Activations and reconstructions are compared as directions: both are scaled to L2 = √d before a per-element MSE, so mse = 2(1 − cos). The variance term is the per-element variance of the scaled activations over the 256 evaluated ones (the AR's own training baseline uses the same definition).

L_h(z) = mse_nrm(h, R(z)) / var_nrm var_nrm = 0.441 FVE = 1 − L_h
L_o(z) = KL( p ‖ p̂(z) ) p̂(z) = F( R(z) · ‖h‖ / ‖R(z)‖ ) patched in at token t “output KL”
S_x(z′) = P(entail | x, z′) − P(contradict | x, z′) for z and every whole-explanation variant
FVE drop(k) = L_h(z_k) − L_h(z) dist(z, z_k) = mse_nrm(R(z), R(z_k)) / var_nrm for each whole-explanation kind k
N(z, z′) = 1[ dist ≤ τ ] P(N=0 | H=1): human-equivalent, NLA-different P(N=1 | H=0): human-different, NLA-equivalent
