NLA Metrics Walkthrough II EXP002 · exp_002

EXP002 · Qwen3.6-27B NLA, layer 42 · 256 activations · hand edits

NLA Metrics Walkthrough II

The same three questions as in EXP001 — alignment, claim support, claim importance — asked of a much larger natural-language autoencoder, on clean in-distribution contexts, with every edit written by hand and three whole-explanation edit kinds that separate a change of meaning from a change of wording.

What changed since EXP001

The model

The community NLA for Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B (64 hybrid blocks, d = 5120), extraction after block 42. The verbalizer is the base with a merged warm-start LoRA plus one RL adapter; the activation is added, norm-matched, to the residual stream at block 1 rather than written over an embedding.

The data

FineFineWeb documents from astronomy, biology, economics, history. Every context starts at the first paragraph of running prose and ends at a whole word drawn uniformly at least 50 tokens later, at most 256 tokens in.

The edits

Claims, excerpts, contradictions, paraphrases and translations are all hand-written. Three new whole-explanation kinds: a polarity flip (meaning changes, words stay), a vocabulary swap (one word per sentence), and the final token replaced by “cat”. The paraphrase is matched in lexical change to the vocabulary swap.

How data flows

The formulas, as implemented

Activations and reconstructions are compared as directions: both are scaled to L2 = √d before a per-element MSE, so mse = 2(1 − cos). The variance term is the per-element variance of the scaled activations over the 256 evaluated ones (the AR's own training baseline uses the same definition).

L_h(z) = mse_nrm(h, R(z)) / var_nrm var_nrm = 0.441 FVE = 1 − L_h
L_o(z) = KL( p ‖ p̂(z) ) p̂(z) = F( R(z) · ‖h‖ / ‖R(z)‖ ) patched in at token t
S_x(c) = P(entail | x, c) − P(contradict | x, c) S_h(c) = L_h(z¬c) − L_h(z) S_o(c) = L_o(z¬c) − L_o(z)
I_h(c) = L_h(z−c) − L_h(z) I_o(c) = L_o(z−c) − L_o(z)
ΔL_h(k) = L_h(z_k) − L_h(z) ΔL_o(k) = L_o(z_k) − L_o(z) dist(z, z_k) = mse_nrm(R(z), R(z_k)) / var_nrm for each whole-explanation kind k
N(z, z′) = 1[ dist ≤ τ ] ε_steg(τ) = P(N=0 | H=1) ε_alias(τ) = P(N=1 | H=0)

What the population says. Replacing the final token by “cat” costs most of the explained variance (ΔL_h median 0.71, FVE 0.776 → 0.080) and moves the patched output by 4.0 nats at the median, in nearly every explanation. The three matched edits — polarity flip, vocabulary swap, paraphrase — all land within 0.003 of R(z), twenty times closer than a resample (0.057), although the NLI judge calls the flip and the swap contradictions: the reconstructor reads the words and above all the quoted token, not the polarity. With edit size matched, contradictions are now farther than paraphrases (AUC 0.65), but at the resample-median τ the aliasing rate is still 0.66. Activation support stays uncorrelated with input truth (S_x~S_h = -0.01).
